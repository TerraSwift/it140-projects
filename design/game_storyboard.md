## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

TODO: Haunted Castle Escape (“Escape from Terra’s Castle”)

**Storyline:**

TODO: In this text-based adventure game, the player is a prisoner locked inside Terra’s castle who must find six hidden keys to unlock the castle gates and escape. The Taskmaster (the villain) guards the gates and will capture anyone who tries to leave without all six keys. To escape, the player must navigate through the eight rooms- The Dungeon (start room), Throne Room, Library, Forbidden Tower, Armory, kitchen, ballroom, and guard tower (villain room. The player must collect six different keys- the ruby key, sapphire key, emerald key, amethyst key, diamond key, and the opal key. If the player enters the guard tower with all six keys, they unlock the gates, trap the taskmaster, and win the game; if they enter before collecting all six keys, they are recaptured and lose. 

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Start room: The Dungeon (No item; Exits: North to throne room)
2. Room: Throne Room (Item: Ruby key; Exits: South to The Dungeon, East to library)
3. Room: Library (Item: Sapphire key; West to throne room, north to Forbidden tower, East to Armory,South to Kitchen)
4. Room: Forbidden Tower (Item: Emerald key; Exits: South to Library)
5. Room: Armory (Item: Amethyst key; Exits: West to Library)
6. Room: Kitchen (Item: Diamond key; Exits: North to library, South to Ballroom, East to Guard Tower)
7. Room: Ballroom (Item: opal key; Exits: North to Kitchen)
8. Villain room: Guard Tower (Villian: The Taskmaster, No items; Exits: West to kitchen)

Add more rooms if your design needs them.
